Privacy policy

homelab rclone is a personal backup tool, used only by its owner, Douglas Viana Coutinho, with his own Google account.

  • What it accesses: only the files it creates itself in the owner's Google Drive (the drive.file scope): the encrypted backup of his home server. It cannot see any other file.
  • What it stores: those backup files, in the owner's own Drive. Nothing is sent anywhere else, and nothing is collected about anyone.
  • Who it is for: the owner alone. No one else signs in to it.
  • Removing access: the owner can revoke it at any time in his Google Account, under Security, Third-party connections.
