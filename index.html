homelab rclone

A personal backup tool, used only by its owner, Douglas Viana Coutinho. It is not offered to anyone else.

Every night, the owner's home server makes an encrypted backup of its own settings and copies it into a folder of the owner's Google Drive, using rclone. It asks Google only for access to the files it creates itself (the drive.file scope); it cannot see or change anything else in the Drive.
